A modular Gameplay Ability System (GAS) plugin for Unreal Engine 5 built to empower gameplay designers with automated ability pipelines and spatial elemental combat.

- Designed and implemented a modular plugin extending GAS to support custom equipment pipelines and automated elemental reactions, applying vector math and collision logic for spatial gameplay mechanics.
- Built custom Slate and UMG editor tools to automate asset generation for GAS, accelerating day-to-day workflow for gameplay designers.
